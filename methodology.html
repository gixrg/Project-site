2. General Methodology & Technical Stack

A structured, multi-phase operational framework designed to extract, prompt, benchmark, and manually reconcile high-granularity cultural graph data.

To systematically bridge the identified data gap without introducing arbitrary narrative additions, we executed a rigorous, data-driven engineering workflow divided into progressive stages:

01

Ontology Module Analysis

Analyzed the formal ICCD/ArCo schema architecture, isolating the core and a-dd (Denotative Description) modules for the physical decomposition via hasPart and spatial boundaries, and the a-cd (Context Description) module for situational tracking and iconographical subject positioning.

02

Baseline SPARQL Auditing

Formulated and executed programmatic graph lookups targeted at the live ministerial SPARQL endpoint. This audit validated the formal existence of the data gap, checking part-whole predicates to establish our target modeling requirements.

03

Semantic Constraint Injection

Constructed a progressive prompt-engineering pipeline (Zero-Shot, Few-Shot Context Anchoring, and Chain-of-Thought Spatial Grids) to force probabilistic LLM engines to stream deterministic, compliant W3C Turtle graph syntaxes.

04

Multi-Model Benchmarking

Submitted identical visual tokens to distinct model architectures (GPT-4o vs Gemini 1.5 Pro) to cross-evaluate output stability, tracking vocabulary convergence, structural accuracy ratios, and prefix mutation vulnerabilities.

05

Human-in-the-Loop Reconciliation

Implemented an expert curation layer to manually inspect, resolve, and unify the graph fragments. This phase successfully corrected the semantic direction of the decomposition and reconciled the positioning tokens into a clean, deployable LOD dataset.
