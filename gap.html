1. Semantic Gap Identification

Sandro Botticelli's Primavera (c. 1480) is an intricate Neoplatonic allegory featuring nine specific mythological characters: Zephyrus, Chloris, Flora, Venus, Cupid, the Three Graces, and Mercury. While institutional cultural registries are excellent at documenting physical properties, they frequently suffer from a severe scarcity of iconographical and denotative metadata.

By auditing the official ArCo Knowledge Graph (IRI: https://w3id.org/arco/resource/HistoricOrArtisticProperty/0900158550), our team discovered a major data void: the graph maps the entire painting as a single monolithic entity, describing its subject with just one flat literal string: "Allegoria della Primavera".

Missing Components The nine independent actors interacting across the orange grove possess zero distinct URI strings or nodes within the official graph.
No Spatial Relations Intricate groupings (such as the interlocking hands of the Three Graces) completely lack spatial localization properties.
Unmappable Narrative Active transformations (e.g., Zephyrus breathing onto Chloris to convert her into Flora) cannot be handled by ArCo's static metadata entry.
