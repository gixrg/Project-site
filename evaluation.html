Tier 1 Audit: Zero-Shot Constraints
// Critique: The Semantic Disconnect

Both engines yielded syntactically correct W3C Turtle representations, demonstrating total adherence to the operational exclusion of conversational preamble. However, a profound Semantic Web Disconnect occurred: lacking ontological anchoring references, both models hallucinated local base URIs.

• Result: Individual characters were correctly segmented, but triples remain isolated from the global Linked Open Data cloud.

Tier 2 Audit: Few-Shot Structural Alignment
// Critique: Contextual Precision Achieved

The injection of structural examples acted as an architectural anchor. By observing the mapping patterns, both models immediately aligned their outputs to the authoritative namespaces of ArCo.

• Result: Zero namespace hallucinations. The structural topology matched our required data blueprint flawlessly.

Tier 3 Audit: Chain-of-Thought Spatial Extraction
// Critique: Advanced Inference vs. Ontological Inversion

Enforcing a normalized grid calculation (100x100) unlocked advanced spatial reasoning. Both models simulated canvas tracking successfully.

ChatGPT: Impeccable relational tracking using the correct core:hasPart property.

Gemini: Outperformed in art-historical naming, but suffered a Critical Ontological Inversion Bug (flipping predicate direction).

// Curation Verdict:

This comparative split justified our Human-in-the-Loop intervention. Our final reconciled dataset combines Gemini's advanced nomenclature with ChatGPT's flawless relational logic, mitigating AI hallucinations to ensure the graph meets the strict requirements of the ArCo ecosystem.
