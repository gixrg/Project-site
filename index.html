Project: Enriching Sandro Botticelli's Primavera within the ArCo Knowledge Graph

Project Abstract

This project explores the collaborative enrichment of cultural heritage metadata through the combined use of Knowledge Graphs (KGs) and Large Language Models (LLMs) within the ArCo framework. Focusing strictly on Sandro Botticelli's Primavera (c. 1482), we execute specialized SPARQL queries to audit data deficiencies in online digital registries. By leveraging zero-shot, few-shot, and chain-of-thought prompt engineering, we generate new validated semantic triples to model individual canvas components, bypassing narrative constraints to capture fine-grained spatial and situational realities.
