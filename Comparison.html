// Technical Audit Logs per Prompting Tier

Granular evaluation of structural behaviors observed during the output streaming phase.

Tier 1 Audit: Zero-Shot Constraints
// Critique: The Semantic Disconnect
Both engines yielded syntactically correct W3C Turtle representations, demonstrating total adherence to the operational exclusion of conversational preamble. However, a profound Semantic Web Disconnect occurred: lacking ontological anchoring references, both models allucinated local base URIs (e.g., http://example.org/ontology/core/).
• Result: Individual characters were correctly segmented into 9 discrete items, but the triples remain isolated from the global Linked Open Data cloud due to broken namespace definitions.
Tier 2 Audit: Few-Shot Structural Alignment
// Critique: Contextual Precision Achieved
The injection of two structural input-output examples acted as an architectural anchor. By observing the mapping patterns of Pallas and The Birth of Venus, both models immediately aligned their outputs to the authoritative namespaces of ArCo (https://w3id.org/arco/ontology/...).
• Result: Zero namespace hallucinations. The structural topology matched our required data blueprint flawlessly, though iconographical depth remained locked at a basic token-label pairing.
Tier 3 Audit: Chain-of-Thought Spatial Extraction
// Critique: Advanced Inference vs. Ontological Inversion
Enforcing a normalized grid calculation ($100 \times 0$) unlocked advanced spatial reasoning. Both models correctly simulated canvas tracking from right to left, generating coordinate literals and handling multi-node overlap.
• ChatGPT (GPT-4o): Exhibited impeccable relational tracking. It resolved the central triad by position (Right, Center, Left Grace) and connected them to the parent node using the proper directional property (core:hasPart).
• Gemini (1.5 Pro): Outperformed ChatGPT in art-historical reasoning, successfully fetching the Renaissance humanistic names of the Graces (Voluptas, Castitas, Pulchritudo). However, it suffered from a critical Ontological Inversion Bug: it flipped the predicate logic, asserting that the individual components contained the parent artwork canvas (ex:Component core:hasPart ex:LaPrimavera_Artwork).
// Curation Verdict:
This comparative split justified our Human-in-the-Loop intervention. Our final reconciled dataset combines Gemini's advanced iconographical nomenclature with ChatGPT's flawless relational directionality.
